Common questions

When does the course start?

We are preparing the first founding group. Paid enrolment opens once arrangements with a licensed training provider are complete, and the waiting list hears the date first.

Do I need IELTS, STEP or TOEFL?

In the official postings we checked, IELTS was always accepted. STEP and TOEFL courses come later.

Is the level check free?

Yes, and no account is needed. The result is a placement group used to organise classes, not an IELTS band.
