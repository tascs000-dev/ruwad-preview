IELTS Academic Band 6 Sprint for graduate programme applicants

Four weeks online: 8 live small-group sessions, short recorded lessons, 6 writing tasks with feedback approved by a qualified teacher, a one-to-one speaking mock and a full mock test.

What you will study

  1. Week 1

    Know the test, plan your essay

    Recorded: test format and Task 2 question types. Live: diagnostic debrief and an essay-planning workshop, then timed listening practice.

  2. Week 2

    Task 1 and speaking

    Recorded: data language and True/False/Not Given. Live: a Task 1 clinic using your drafts, and speaking practice in small groups.

  3. Week 3

    Common errors and discussion

    Recorded: headings, matching and paragraph development. Live: your group's top 10 errors, and Speaking Part 3 discussion. Your one-to-one speaking mock.

  4. Week 4

    Timed practice, mock and plan

    A full mock test. Live: a timed essay with instant feedback, then the mock debrief, a test-day plan and English for programme applications.

What you get

  • A free placement test before you enrol. If the course doesn't suit your level, we tell you.
  • 8 live sessions of 90 minutes (12 hours) in a group of 8 to 10, focused on practice and feedback on your own work.
  • Short recorded lessons on the test and strategies, to rewatch any time.
  • 6 writing tasks: AI drafts the notes, and a qualified teacher reviews them and approves every assessment before you see it.
  • A one-to-one speaking mock with a teacher (15 minutes) in week 3, plus recorded speaking practice.
  • A full mock test at the end, feedback per skill and a plan for after the course.
  • A session on English for graduate programme applications: CV and interview.

Four weeks mainly helps learners who are already close to their target, by fixing technique, timing and the most common writing and speaking errors. If you are further away, we suggest a longer route. We do not guarantee any score.
