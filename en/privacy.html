Draft written against the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). It needs legal review before any real data is collected. Not legal advice. The legal entity name, CR number and contact address are added once registered.

Who we are

We don't ask for or keep copies of national IDs unless the law requires it. We don't sell your data or send marketing without your consent, and you can stop it at any time.

AI tools

If we use AI tools for first-pass writing feedback, we send only the text, never your name, number or email, and a qualified teacher reviews the final assessment.

Who sees your data and where it is kept

The Ruwad team, the teachers assigned to your group, and the licensed training provider the course runs through (named before enrolment). Hosting, live-class and payment providers and data locations will be listed here once chosen. We prefer hosting in Saudi Arabia or the GCC; if data leaves the Kingdom we apply the safeguards the law requires and tell you.

How long we keep it

Waiting-list data: until the course you registered for starts or 12 months, whichever comes first, then deleted unless you enrol. Study data: for the course and as long as regulations require afterwards. (Proposed periods, to be confirmed after legal review.)

Your rights

  • To know how we use your data and get a copy.
  • To correct or complete it.
  • To ask us to delete it when we no longer need it.
  • To withdraw your consent at any time.

We answer requests within 30 days. You can also complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

Security and incidents

We protect your data with technical and organisational controls. If a breach is likely to harm you, we notify the regulator within 72 hours and tell you without delay.

Age

Our services are for people aged 18 and over.

This prototype

In this version all data stays in your browser and never reaches us. You can delete it by clearing this site's data in your browser.
