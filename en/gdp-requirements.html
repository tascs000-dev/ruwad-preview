What do graduate programmes ask for?

English requirements as written in official postings we read on each organisation's own site, with a link to every source. Requirements change every year, so check the posting before you apply. We add other programmes only after checking them at the official source.

Last checked: 8 Nov 2026

What this tells us

  • In these official postings, IELTS was accepted every time a test was named.
  • The scores listed range from IELTS 5.5 to 6.5.
  • PIF accepts only certificates issued in 2025 or later, so watch your test date.
  • SAL's posting also lists STEP among accepted scores.

Organisations that name no test

They ask for "fluent English" and check it with their own tests and interviews:

  • SAIB: Graduate Development Program ("advanced English language skills") (source)
  • Al Rajhi Bank: Graduate Development Program GDP10 ("fluent English") (source)
  • Tamasuk: Graduate Development Program ("proficient in English") (source)
  • Saudi Aramco: graduate hiring (source)
  • SABIC: early-career programmes (source)

Everything on this page was read on the organisation's official site. Tell us about a missing programme or a changed requirement through the contact page.
