About Ruwad

Ruwad is a young Saudi academy in Riyadh that connects learning English to your career ambitions.

Why Ruwad

Many graduates know the programme or job they want, but not exactly which English test it requires, what score, or how long it will take to get there. We start from that question.

How we work

  • We check English requirements at each organisation's official source.
  • We write original practice material and never copy official test questions.
  • We use AI where it genuinely helps, such as quick feedback and planning, while important assessments stay with a qualified instructor.
  • We measure every student's progress and share it honestly.

The team

Ruwad was founded by a Saudi founder working with a small team. We are selecting our first instructors for the pilot group and will publish each instructor's profile and qualifications before courses begin.
