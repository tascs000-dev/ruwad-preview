STEP (Standardized Test of English Proficiency)

STEP is the English test set by the Education & Training Evaluation Commission. Many Saudi organisations ask for it in admissions and hiring. Knowing the score your target organisation asks for is the first step. Some programmes list STEP among accepted scores (for example, SAL's "Towsal" posting lists IELTS 5.5 and STEP 75). We tell you when STEP suits your programme.

About the test

Run by
Education & Training Evaluation Commission (Qiyas)
Scores
0 to 100
Sections
Multiple choice: reading, structure, listening, written analysis
Asked for by
Saudi universities, some graduate programmes and scholarships

English requirements of graduate programmes

Common questions

What score do I need?

It depends on the organisation and programme and can change each year. We check the requirement against the organisation's official source while preparing your plan, not against numbers passed around online.

How do I book the test itself?

You book the test through the official Qiyas portal, not through Ruwad. We help you choose a date that fits your plan.
